04  /  How We Coach

Correct first.
Load second.

01

Assess

Every member is screened before they load a bar. We look at how you actually move, and at what your history rules in or out.

02

Prescribe

You leave with a written block — sets, reps, loads and progression rules. Nothing about your week is left to guesswork.

03

Adjust

Blocks are reviewed every six weeks against real numbers. If something is not working, it changes.
