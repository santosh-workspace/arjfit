01  /  Programs

Six ways in.
One set of standards.

Every programme runs on written blocks, capped group sizes and coaches who correct your position before adding weight.

02  /  Choose

Not sure which one?

Most members combine two — usually strength twice a week with conditioning or mobility alongside. Your first session includes a conversation about what actually fits your week.

Barbell Strength Conditioning Personal Training Hybrid Athlete Mobility & Recovery Foundations

01  /  Small group · 60 min

Barbell Strength

The core of the gym. A linear or block programme built around the squat, deadlift, press and row, with accessory work chosen for your weak points rather than a template.

  • Movement screen before you load anything
  • Written block reviewed every six weeks
  • Calibrated plates and competition bars
  • Suitable from first barbell session onward

Best for

Anyone who wants to get measurably stronger and have the numbers to prove it.

02  /  Small group · 45 min

Conditioning

Engine work that is actually programmed. Intervals, sled pushes, echo bike and loaded carries, structured across the week so hard days are hard and easy days stay easy.

  • Effort prescribed by pace or heart rate
  • Scaled to your current capacity, not the room average
  • Low-impact options throughout
  • Pairs with strength without wrecking recovery

Best for

Lifters who are strong but gas out, and anyone rebuilding general fitness.

03  /  1:1 · 55 min

Personal Training

One coach, one plan, one set of priorities. The most direct route if you are returning from injury, working around a medical constraint, or stuck at the same numbers for months.

  • Full assessment and history intake
  • Programme written specifically for you
  • Session-by-session load adjustment
  • Direct messaging with your coach between sessions

Best for

Returning from a break or injury, or past the point a group can serve you.

04  /  Small group · 75 min

Hybrid Athlete

Strength and endurance in the same block, sequenced so they stop competing with each other. For people training for an event while refusing to get weaker.

  • Concurrent programming with real periodisation
  • Run, row and bike work built into the plan
  • Deload weeks scheduled, not improvised
  • Event-week tapering

Best for

Runners, cyclists and obstacle-race entrants who also want to lift.

05  /  Small group · 40 min

Mobility & Recovery

Not a stretch class. Loaded end-range work, positional drills and breathing, aimed at the specific ranges your lifts need. The session that keeps the other four available to you.

  • Joint-by-joint assessment
  • Loaded mobility, not passive stretching
  • Desk-posture and hip-opening focus
  • Ideal on rest days

Best for

Desk-bound members and anyone whose squat depth is a running argument.

06  /  4-week intro · 2×/week

Foundations

The on-ramp. Four weeks of small-group coaching that takes you from never having trained to confidently running a session on your own. Every new member is welcome to start here.

  • Absolute beginners only — no experience assumed
  • Learn the six core movement patterns
  • Gym etiquette and equipment walkthrough
  • Finishes with your first written programme

Best for

Anyone who has been putting off joining a gym because they do not know where to start.
